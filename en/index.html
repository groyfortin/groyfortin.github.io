Guillaume Roy-Fortin

I am a teaching professor of mathematics at the Ecole de technologie superieure.

Previously, I was a Ralph Boas Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Northwestern. In 2015, I completed my Ph.D. at Universite de Montreal under the generous supervision of Iosif Polterovich. I also have a background in psychology.

Research

My research interests fall into two general directions.

First, I study eigenfunctions of elliptic operators on Riemannian manifolds, in particular the properties of their nodal sets.

I am also interested in the mathematical aspects underlying machine learning.
