I am a teaching professor of mathematics at the École de technologie supérieure.

Research

My research interests fall into two general directions.

First, I study eigenfunctions of elliptic operators on Riemannian manifolds, in particular the properties of their nodal sets.

I am also interested in the mathematical aspects underlying machine learning.
